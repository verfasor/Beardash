Quick start

  1. Get an API key. In Bear, open Settings → API and create a key. API access needs an upgraded Bear plan.
  2. Connect your blog. Open the dashboard, enter your blog's subdomain and paste the key.
  3. Write. Start a new post in Markdown with a live preview. Paste or drop images to upload them.
  4. Publish or schedule. Choose Publish to post now, or pick a date and time and Bear publishes it for you.
  5. Add more blogs. Click your blog's name at the top left, then Add a blog. Each blog has its own key.
  6. Keep it safe. Open the menu at the bottom of the sidebar, then Keys & data. Set a passphrase, or export an encrypted backup to use Beardash on another device.

Press ? in the dashboard to see keyboard shortcuts. The full guide is under Docs in the sidebar.

Privacy

Beardash has no accounts and no database. There's nothing to sign up for and nowhere for us to keep your data.

  • Your API keys stay in your browser. They're saved in your browser's storage, and you can encrypt them with a passphrase. They're never stored on our servers.
  • Requests pass straight through. Beardash talks to Bear through a small Cloudflare Worker that forwards each request and keeps nothing. It has no logs, no cookies and no database.
  • Your writing stays local. Revision history and unsaved drafts are kept in your browser too. Your posts live on Bear, as they always have.
  • No tracking. Beardash uses no analytics, no ad trackers and no cookies.
  • Abuse protection. Cloudflare briefly counts requests per IP address to stop abuse. Beardash doesn't store or see those counts.

Clearing your browser's data for this site removes everything Beardash saved. Your blog on Bear isn't affected.

Beardash isn't affiliated with Bear Blog. Bear's own privacy policy covers your blog and your Bear account.
